
- **Door opening.** Ground-navigating game mobs can open wooden doors (`setCanOpenDoors` plus `OpenDoorGoal`, which leaves them open), so a door's state when a game starts doesn't cut zones apart.
- **Window-aware spawning.** The spawner only picks a profile that fits the marker's window. It uses `GameWorld.canSpawn`, which compares the profile's entity-type size, rounded up to whole blocks, with the window opening. An iron golem (2x3) is never sent to a 1x2 or 2x2 window.
- **Health and speed per profile.** A game mob gets the round's zombie health (`RoundPlan.zombieHealth`) times its profile's `health_multiplier`, and the round's speed times `speed_multiplier` (both default 1). The shipped `hellstorm:hellhound` has 0.33: hounds come fast (1.5× speed, 1.5× spawn rate) and in a pack, so each should drop in a third of the shots a zombie of that round takes (at round 5, 27 HP against 82.5). Vanilla caps max health at 1024, which zombies reach at round 30 and hounds at round 42; from there a hound is as tough as a zombie. GameTest `aHoundHasAThirdOfAZombiesHealth`.
- **Navigation per profile.** `"navigation": "flow"` (the default) steers on the game's flow fields (see `zombie-pathfinding.md`). `"navigation": "vanilla"` keeps the mob's own pathfinding and re-paths to its target every 10 ticks. Mobs whose navigation isn't walk-based (spiders on walls, fliers, swimmers) always use their own, whatever the profile says. Game mobs get a follow range of 128 so vanilla navigation finds players across a map.
- **Vanilla navigation's search budget.** Vanilla pathfinding visits at most 16 nodes per block of follow range. It sizes that budget when the mob is constructed, from its type's default range: 560 nodes for a zombie and 256 for a wolf. Raising the attribute later doesn't change it. `AiInjector` scales the budget to the 128-block range (2048 nodes) with `setMaxVisitedNodesMultiplier`. Without that, a mob on vanilla navigation (a `vanilla` profile, the flow navigator's stall rescue, or a mob off the flow graph) stopped at the wall nearest a far player when the route went the long way round. GameTests `aVanillaNavigatingZombieWalksOutOfADeadEndToAFarPlayer` and `aVanillaNavigatingWolfWalksOutOfADeadEndToAFarPlayer` cover this. Targeting has no range or sight limit. `aGameZombieChasesAFarPlayerItCannotSee` and `aGameHoundChasesAFarPlayerItCannotSee` check that a game mob 46 blocks away, behind a wall, targets the player at once and reaches them. `aZombieTurnsToTheFarPlayerWhenTheNearOneStopsBeingATarget` checks that a zombie switches targets within half a second.

## Dismemberment and crawlers (2026-10-23)

- **Where a shot lands** (`mob/BodyPart`). TacZ tells a headshot from where its bullet struck; its hurt events carry only the flag, so `tacz/mixin/EntityKineticBulletHitMixin` notes the hit point as TacZ's `onHitEntity` starts (`tacz/BulletHits`, keyed by bullet), and the survived-hit handler reads it back. `BodyPart.at` places the point on the zombie's model, 32 pixels tall: legs to pixel 12, body and arms to 24, the head above (or wherever TacZ called a headshot). Across, the hitbox (0.6 wide, narrower than the model) is scaled to the model's 16 pixels: the middle 8 are the body, the 4 each side an arm; a leg is the left or right half. Left and right are the zombie's own, from its body's yaw. JUnit `BodyPartTest`.
- **Limbs come off** (`mob/Dismemberment`). A non-lethal TacZ hit on a game `minecraft:zombie`'s arm or leg adds its damage to that limb; once a limb has taken a fifth of the zombie's max health (`LIMB_SHARE`, 0.2: one pistol shot in round 1, several later), it comes off, with rotten flesh bits and a squish. The head and body never come off. A crawler loses nothing more.
- **A lost leg makes a crawler** at half speed (`CRAWL_SPEED`, 0.5).
- **The crawler tag** (`mob/Limbs`). `ForgeData` holds `hellstorm_limbs` (a byte of lost limbs) and `hellstorm_crawler: {speed_multiplier: <f>}`. The tag does it all: on both sides `EntityEvent.Size` lays the hitbox prone (0.6 by 0.6); on the server, when a tagged zombie joins the level or gets the tag, a `MULTIPLY_TOTAL` speed modifier of `speed_multiplier - 1`. Both are saved with the zombie. `LimbsPacket` tells players the limbs and the crawl when one changes or they start seeing the zombie.
- **The crawler profile** `hellstorm:crawler`: `minecraft:zombie` with `{ForgeData:{hellstorm_crawler:{speed_multiplier:0.5f}}}`, weight 0, so only wave events or scripts pick it (as `hellhound` is).
- **Drawing them** (`client/ZombieLimbs`). Hellstorm replaces vanilla's zombie renderer with one that matches it, but hides lost limbs, and lays a crawler face down along its heading (as vanilla lays a crawling player: -90 degrees about its side, then 1 block back and 0.3 up), its arms reaching out past its head in turn as it moves and its legs dragging. Armor drawn on a lost limb still shows. Another mod that replaces the zombie renderer would undo this, or this would undo it.
- **Tests.** GameTests `DismembermentGameTests`: half a limb's share takes nothing, the rest takes the right arm, an armless zombie still walks, the body and head never come off, a shot leg makes a crawler with the prone hitbox and half speed, a crawler loses nothing more, and the lost limbs are saved; the crawler profile crawls from the start at half a walker's speed; and a fake player's real TacZ AK, aimed at a zombie's legs, takes a leg off and makes it crawl. How it looks is checked in game.
