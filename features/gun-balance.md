# Gun balance

## Goal

TacZ's default guns are balanced for TacZ's own combat, not for zombies rounds. Out of the box, Hellstorm evens them out: sidearms and shotguns with tiny magazines no longer run dry after four reloads, and guns with weak damage hit a little harder or carry more ammo. Any datapack can change a gun's balance or add balance for guns from other gun packs.

## Data shape

One file per gun, at `data/<gun namespace>/hellstorm/gun_balance/<gun path>.json`. The file's id is the gun's id: `data/tacz/hellstorm/gun_balance/m1911.json` balances `tacz:m1911`.

```json
{ "damage_multiplier": 1.35, "reserve_multiplier": 2.5 }
```

- `damage_multiplier` (default 1) scales every hit from the gun.
- `reserve_multiplier` (default 1) scales the reserve: a gun's reserve is its magazine times `players.reserve_magazines` times this. The setting still scales every gun.
- A gun with no file plays as TacZ made it.

## Implementation

- `data/GunBalance` (record and codec) and `data/GunBalances` (reload listener on `hellstorm/gun_balance`, refreshed on `/reload`).
- **Reserve.** `TaczBridge.createGun` sizes the dummy reserve with `GunBalance.reserve`. Every gun Hellstorm hands out (starting guns, wall-buys, the crate, the upgrader's results, the downed pistol, `/hs give`) goes through it, so the max reserve is fixed on the stack when the gun is made, and refills fill back to it.
- **Damage.** `TaczEvents.onGunDamage` multiplies `EntityHurtByGunEvent.Pre`'s base amount on the server. It reads the gun id from the event, so it applies to any gun with that id, however it was made, and follows `/reload` at once. The upgrader's and perks' multipliers stack with it.
- **The defaults** are generated by `tools/balance_guns.py` from TacZ's default gun pack (`run/tacz/tacz_default_gun`, unpacked by TacZ on first launch). Rerun it after changing its model. It also writes `features/gun-balance-table.md`.

## The default model

The AK-47 is the baseline: 9 damage, 30 rounds, 120 in reserve, so a full load deals 1350.

1. **A shot's damage.** TacZ's `damage`. For shotguns this is the whole shot, which TacZ splits evenly across its pellets (`EntityKineticBullet.applyShotgunDamageSpread`), and the model counts 70% of it as landing.
2. **Damage per second.** The shot's damage times the rpm, with guns that have no automatic mode capped at 450 rpm (how fast anyone clicks).
3. **Damage.** A gun below its class's floor gets more damage, up to 1.35 times: pistols 60, SMGs 85, rifles 85, machine guns 95, shotguns 90, snipers 75 damage per second.
4. **Reserve.** Sized so a full load deals the class's share of the AK-47's: 1.0 for pistols, rifles, and shotguns, 1.1 for SMGs, 1.25 for machine guns, 0.9 for snipers. A gun still below its floor after the damage boost gets more on top, in proportion to what it lacks. The multiplier stays between 0.75 and 8, so the strongest guns lose at most a quarter of their reserve.
5. **Launchers** do their damage by explosion, which the model doesn't measure: the RPG-7 gets twice its reserve (9 rockets) and the M320 three times (13 grenades), set by hand.
6. **The minigun** is fed from the inventory, not a magazine, so dummy reserve doesn't apply. It isn't balanced.

The result for every gun is in `features/gun-balance-table.md`. Pistols carry two to four times their old reserve (an M1911 goes from 28 to 119), shotguns two to eight times (an M870 from 20 to 40, a double-barrel from 8 to 64), and the ten weakest (Glock 17, M9A4, Mk 23, Lonetrail, Rhino 357, Taurus 943, both double-barrels, M16A4, Springfield 1873) hit 35% harder.

- The `hellstorm:gun_balance` datapack files above.
- `players.reserve_magazines` (4) still scales every reserve.

## Verification

- JUnit `GunBalanceTest`: an empty file changes nothing; the reserve follows `reserve_magazines`; every shipped file parses, changes something, and stays in the model's bounds.
- GameTest `GunBalanceGameTests`: with the mod's own datapack loaded, an M1911 carries 119 in reserve, an M870 40, and the AK-47 its stock 120; a Glock 17 hit of 6 becomes 8.1 on the server, an AK-47 hit stays 9, and the client's copy of the event isn't scaled.
- In game (not yet done): play several rounds with a pistol, a shotgun, and a rifle and check none runs dry long before the others.
